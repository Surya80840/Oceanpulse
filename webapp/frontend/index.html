    About the forecast

    • Model 1 - 30-day rolling ocean state; real data only from ERDDAP (SST, chlorophyll) and Copernicus Marine (SSH, salinity, O2). Refreshes one day at a time.
    • Model 2 - 369 logistic-regression species classifiers trained on CalCOFI + iNaturalist + NOAA WCGBT observations.
    • Cells without any training observation (n_obs = 0) are drawn dimmer; interpret predictions with caution there.
